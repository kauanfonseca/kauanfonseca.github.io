Background

I hold a PhD in Ecology and Evolution from the Universidade do Estado do Rio de Janeiro (UERJ), Brazil. My dissertation dealt with karst streams in the Brazilian savanna, in Mato Grosso (see map below), where I investigated how biodiversity modulates the structure and functioning of these ecosystems. I have also worked on environmental consultancy projects in the Amazon and Atlantic Forest regions, evaluating the effects of mining and hydropower development on aquatic communities. I am currently an associate fellow at the Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas do Pantanal (INPP), supported by a CNPq PCI-DB fellowship, and a postdoctoral researcher at UERJ.

Where I worked

My study area in region of Mato Grosso, Brazil. Circles are sampling sites area, squares are logistical support (field station and laboratory), the triangle is the state capital.

Research interests

  • Structure and functioning of aquatic and freshwater ecosystems
  • Carbon and nutrient cycling, and the role of organisms in driving it
  • Karst aquatic ecosystems and calcium carbonate precipitation
  • Stream ecosystem responses to environmental change
  • Field campaigns paired with manipulative experiments

Where I want to take this

Two directions I want to build toward: long-term monitoring of the streams I currently sample campaign by campaign, and scaling the questions beyond single catchments.
